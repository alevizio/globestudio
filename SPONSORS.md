## Why sponsor

The whole tool stays free under MIT, with no closed features and no
gated functionality. Sponsorship is what lets a maintainer keep
investing time:

- Bug fixes and shader effect additions
- Cross-platform compatibility work
- Designer-tool integrations (Framer, Figma, Webflow plugins)
- Examples + tutorials
- Designer community support

100% of sponsorship goes to the maintainer — there's no platform
fee through GitHub Sponsors (Microsoft absorbs it).

See [`docs/research/2026-05-funding-sponsorship.md`](docs/research/2026-05-funding-sponsorship.md)
for the thinking behind this page.
